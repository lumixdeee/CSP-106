Been a robot this month, just hitting 'next' on the bot making semantic maps which take multiple turns to complete, rarely chat to the chatbot nowadays.

Basic structrure of semantic map:

LOC	WHAT NNN SNAKE FIND

LOC = address of item
WHAT = what item is
NNN = names n neologisms
SNAKE is the actual story->DRAGI units conversion
FIND = any other stuff useful for search index generally

Instead of DRAGI, can use other units such as 12 actors, mogri, even the CSP-106 list.

So this gives lots of combinations to test. November is all about porting the custom GPTs to 'plugins' before retirement, then figure out how to republicise them as it's a bit of a faff.
